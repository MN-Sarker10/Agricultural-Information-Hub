Crop Details

Crops are central to human civilization and livelihoods. In Bangladesh, rice is the primary staple covering most farmland, alongside major crops like wheat, jute, tea, potatoes, and various seasonal vegetables.

By Growing Season

Crops grown during the monsoon season (roughly mid-March to mid-October) benefit from high humidity and natural rain. Major seasonal varieties include Aus, Aman rice, and jute.
