Crop Details & Seasons

Crops are essential plants cultivated for food, fiber, and fuel. In Bangladesh, agriculture heavily relies on seasonal cycles—ranging from monsoon crops to winter harvests.

Monsoon Season

Mid-July to Mid-November (Kharif)

Cultivated primarily during the rainy season. Major crops in this period include Aus and Aman rice varieties, along with jute, requiring abundant water and sunlight for optimal growth.

Read Detailed Guide
Winter Season

Mid-November to Mid-March (Rabi)

Grown in cooler temperatures with controlled irrigation. Key crops include Boro rice, wheat, pulses (lentils), mustard, and a wide variety of fresh winter vegetables.
